Good to know

Before you order

How does pricing work?

Cake prices vary based on the design. The menu prices are starting points; Mika will confirm your total after reviewing the details.

When is payment due?

A non-refundable deposit is due within 24 hours and is applied to your balance. The remaining balance is due at pickup.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Pickup is in 19124 Northwood, Philadelphia. Delivery is available for a distance-based fee.

How much notice do you need?

Orders placed with less than 5 days’ notice are subject to a rush fee.

Are there any design limits?

Mika does not offer fondant, alcohol, profanity, edible images, zodiac signs, real flowers, or burn cakes. She does her best to recreate inspiration photos, but every cake is custom-made, so exact replicas cannot be guaranteed.
